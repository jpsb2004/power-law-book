## Caveats

- One window of about a year. These are the realised paths of a book chosen with hindsight about which themes ran; they are not estimates of future return or risk.
- Scenario impacts are the stated shocks in data/scenarios.json (judgements, not estimates). The NLR stand-in takes NLR's shocks; cash takes 0.
- ENB is a point estimate from a sample covariance on weekly returns; the range is a moving-block bootstrap (5th–95th percentile).
- Risk shares and ENB leave out lines with fewer than 40 weeks of history: RARA11.SA.
- No line here is a recommendation to buy or sell (CVM Res. 20).
